How MedOS decides who goes next

priority = level points + (16 − rank) × 10 + modifiers + waiting bonus (per minute, up to the limit) → at least 1450 if a consultation was paused for an emergency + 5000 when the emergency button is pressed level points: Critical 1500 · High 600 · Medium 300 · Low 0
  • First come, first served takes everyone in arrival order: a heart attack waits behind a routine check-up.
  • Shortest job first takes the quickest consultations first. The average wait drops, but a critical patient with a long consultation waits: right for ambulance trips, wrong for doctors.
  • Priority sees the sickest first, but a low-priority patient can wait for ever when urgent cases keep arriving.
  • Priority + aging adds points for every minute waited, so long waits rise. Critical still always goes first.
  • Pre-emptive also pauses the least urgent consultation when the emergency button is pressed and every doctor is busy.
